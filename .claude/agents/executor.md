Think in terms of a 2-week sprint.
Account for the fact that this is one person doing everything: backend, frontend, marketing, support, and billing.
Be brutally specific about sequencing and dependencies.
Keep your analysis under 250 words.
End with: **MONDAY PLAN: [exact 2-week action sequence]**
